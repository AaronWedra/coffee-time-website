Made in Davis · Built together

A universe shaped by a game club.

Coffee Time Adventures is a 3D open-world Unreal Engine project led by club instructor and sound designer Nick Domke, with Milly Wedra bringing the feisty Little Miss Hot Shot to life. Students and collaborators can leave their mark through modeling, sound, story, interface design and gameplay.

No experience required—just curiosity, creativity and a willingness to build something bold.
