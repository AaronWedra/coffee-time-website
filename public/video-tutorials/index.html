CHAPTER 01

Get started

Know the table before the clocks begin.

01Opening the box and setup

Lay out the game board, cards and player pieces. Choose Coffee Time heroes and the players controlling King Tea and Roamer. Match each hero with their Player Card and unique movement die.

CHAPTER 02

Move & battle

Travel the Five Worlds and survive what you meet.

02AHero movement

Roll the die shown on your Player Card and apply any additional movement from your hero ability or current World perk. Always draw a Journey Card after moving.

02BTeam Path crossroads

Landing on a Team Path Crossroads immediately places the hero on the Team Path. On the next turn, choose the purple Team Path or continue along the original route.

03Battling a common enemy

Win: place the enemy card on the bottom of the Journey Deck. Lose: reduce health by one and discard the enemy. Tie: both sides roll again.

04Health depleted

Each lost common-enemy battle reduces health by one. At zero health, return to Start and fully replenish health. Temperature is not replenished.

05Team Path powers

Heroes on a circular Team Path space or in the Activation Chamber share their unique Team Path powers. Read each active power aloud. If Donette is on the Activation Button, the entire board gains Team Path power sharing.

06The Activation Chamber

Only one hero occupies the Activation Button. A lone hero entering the Chamber takes it immediately; an incoming hero may push another hero off. A hero beginning a turn in the Chamber may also take over. If the button holder is defeated, the remaining heroes choose a replacement by consensus.

CHAPTER 03

Cards & powers

Use the Journey Deck to change the game.

07Temperature, freezing and sharing heat

Ice reduces temperature by two. At zero, a hero is frozen: they cannot move, draw, battle or use cards except Fire, and cannot be attacked by Roamer or Guards. A frozen button holder keeps their activation power. At the beginning of a turn, coffee heroes may transfer heat one-for-one. Donette has no temperature gauge and cannot freeze.

08Card Protector

Thermos begins with Card Protector and may keep or share it. Place it horizontally across one item; that item is protected continuously from Roamer’s grave-robbing and the Duchess.

09Earth card

Offer Earth once to avoid a Roamer attack or prevent Roamer from stealing an item after a successful grave-robbing roll.

10Health card

Use Health at any point in your turn to replenish before reaching zero—or use it after defeat to recover instead of returning to Start.

11Honey Bee Queen

Dotty sends your hero directly to the Activation Chamber. Use immediately or save for a later turn, then draw another Journey Card after arriving.

12Sprinkles card

Transform Sprinkles into any usable or shareable card. Once declared, the choice is permanent. Declare immediately or wait until any player’s turn.

13Wind Feather

Create a gust that lets heroes trade cards without limits. Card Protector may also move between players and cover a new item. Use once.

14Sleepy Time Tea

Turn the clocks back six hours, or risk a d12 roll to determine the hours reversed. King Tea loses the most recently gained hours. Use once.

15Team Warp

Move to the next Team Path crossroads; if both have been passed, move to the Activation Chamber. Already on the Team Path? Take an extra roll. Draw after every move.

16Sugar Cube

Roll again immediately after drawing, or save the card and use it at the end of a later turn to take a second turn.

17Duchess enchantments

The Duchess may send you to Start, discard unprotected items, freeze you, or summon Full Moon Roamer. Little Miss Hot Shot is fast enough to discard every Duchess Enchantment without effect.

CHAPTER 04

Roamer

Move, attack and grave-rob across every path.

18Playing Roamer

Draw a Roamer Card to spawn, then roll d4 + 2 for movement across any hero path. Attack one reachable hero with a d12. At turn end, grave-rob any hero by winning a battle; protected items are safe. In Crumpet World, Full Moon Roamer rolls two d12s and adds them.

On the Activation Button, steal one unprotected item from every player without battling. Trumpet can once spawn Roamer anywhere with Full Moon power, or once create a full-round defense that prevents attacks on the Realm of Time.

CHAPTER 05

King Tea

Advance the clocks and command the Royal Guards.

19Playing King Tea

Choose whether to move or battle first. To move, roll d12 and advance World Clocks left-to-right; reaching 12 advances King Tea into the next world. Royal Guards can attack any hero with d12, fully draining health on a win; a defeated Guard is removed for the game.

Once per game, Overtime doubles the hours just rolled. While in Tea World, Ice Storm may reduce every hero’s temperature by one at the end of every King Tea turn.

CHAPTER 06

Win the game

Only one side controls the final hour.

20AKing Tea wins

If King Tea reaches 12—Tea Time—in Tea World, coffee is vanquished, the Five Worlds fall out of balance and tea and crumpets reign supreme.

20BThe Coffee Time heroes win

Defeat every Royal Guard, reach the Activation Chamber and wait until the beginning of the next turn to challenge King Tea. Both roll d12. If the hero loses, return to Start. If King Tea loses, the heroes save the Five Worlds.
