Rules and gameplay

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a hero begin a turn?

Choose whether to share an item card or heat. Then choose whether to use item cards, announce each card and follow its instructions. Finally, follow the movement instructions on your Player Card and draw a Journey Card after moving.

What happens when a hero freezes?

At zero heat, a hero is frozen until reheated by a Fire card or heat shared by a teammate. Frozen players cannot draw Journey Cards, battle or use cards other than Fire.
