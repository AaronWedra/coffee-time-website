Quick rulings

Keep the adventure moving.

These answers reflect the first edition released in 2023. For step-by-step gameplay, visit the complete 20-lesson guide.

How to play
01How is a complete turn played for heroes and villains?

Heroes:

  1. Choose whether to share one item card and/or heat.
  2. Choose whether to use one or more item cards. Announce each card, follow its instructions and discard it when required.
  3. Follow the movement instructions on the Player Card. If in the Activation Chamber, apply its special rules.
  4. Draw a Journey Card. Battle enemies, immediately use or keep item cards, and follow any Duchess Enchantment.

Roamer:

  1. Choose whether to use one function of Trumpet.
  2. Move according to the Player Card.
  3. Attack a reachable hero if desired.
  4. Attempt grave-robbing.
  5. Return Roamer to the grave.

Roamer is already undead and never dies. He plays every round, may grave-rob after losing a battle, and discards rather than uses stolen cards.

King Tea:

  1. Choose whether to move or attack first.
  2. When moving, roll the correct die, advance the clocks and decide immediately whether to use Overtime.
  3. When attacking, follow the Royal Guard battle rules.
  4. While in Tea World, Ice Storm may be used at the end of the turn.
02What if Roamer is the first player to start?

If Roamer wins the opening high roll and acts before any hero has moved, he may use that first turn to attempt to grave-rob Thermos’s Card Protector. This is the only point in the game when Card Protector itself can be taken.

03How does King Tea move through the Five Worlds?

Roll the 12-sided die and move the clock hands that many cumulative hours, beginning in Coffee World and continuing through Donut, Warp, Crumpet and Tea Worlds.

Example: from 11:00 in Coffee World, a roll of 3 reaches 12:00/Tea Time there and continues to 2:00 in Donut World. The King Tea piece can remain on its Realm of Time home space.

King Tea needs 60 cumulative hours to win, plus any hours the heroes reverse with Sleepy Time Tea. Reaching hour 12 immediately enters the next World at hour 0.

04When can a hero share heat or item cards?

At the beginning of the hero’s turn, before rolling to move. Heat transfers one increment at a time: each increment given is removed from the sharing hero.

05If I land on a Team Path Crossroads, am I already on the Team Path?

Yes. You immediately activate your Team Path Power. Every circular space and the Activation Chamber count as Team Path spaces. On your next turn, choose the purple Team Path or continue on your original path and give up further shared Team Path powers.

06What does the 0 on the ten-sided die mean?

The zero face is valued as 10.

07Can a hero be attacked on their Start space?

Yes, technically—but their health remains full there, so the attack has no useful effect.

08Can Earth stop Roamer’s Activation Power?

No. When Roamer uses his Activation Power, he steals one item of his choice from every player. This is not a normal grave-robbing attempt, so Earth cannot prevent it. Card Protector still protects the item it covers.
