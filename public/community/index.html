Meet the creators

The original tabletop community.

Aaron Wedra and Stevie Price created the original Coffee Time board game alongside a growing team of artists, editors, musicians and dedicated playtesters. That tabletop community established the characters, worlds and shared spirit now expanding into the video game.

Aaron describes the heart of the game simply: “Dive into our characters’ worlds and help Cup Joe and his friends stop King Tea from making it Tea Time Forever—or sound the trumpets and join the crumpets.”
