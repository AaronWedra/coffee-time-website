Five years of imagination, playtesting, art, music, friendship and very strong opinions about coffee brought the Realm of Time to life.

Meet the creators

Built over five unforgettable years.

Aaron Wedra and Stevie Price created Coffee Time alongside a growing team of artists, editors, musicians, developers and dedicated playtesters.

Aaron describes the heart of the game simply: “Dive into our characters’ worlds and help Cup Joe and his friends stop King Tea from making it Tea Time Forever—or sound the trumpets and join the crumpets.”
