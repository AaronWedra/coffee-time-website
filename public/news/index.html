2025 · SAN DIEGO

Back at Comic-Con.

Coffee Time returned to the legendary San Diego International Comic-Con, surrounded by established creators and first-time game makers alike.

When the team first attended in 2023, Coffee Time was just starting out. Two years later, more than 600 games were being played by families, fans and nonprofits across the country—and overseas. Every demo player left smiling, with a sticker of their favorite character.

2025

Development update

Coffee Time Adventures video game

Aaron Wedra, Milly Wedra and Nick Domke are bringing the universe to life in its first full-scale video game. Little Miss Hot Shot will explore five worlds, unlock powers, battle crumpet hordes and uncover the plans of King Tea and the mysterious Duchess.

Developed in Davis, California, the project is a labor of love, creativity and teamwork—with a free playable demo already available.

NOV 2024

Convention diary

Invited to San Diego International Comic-Con

Day 1: A slow opening transformed into nonstop, back-to-back battles between coffee heroes and tea villains.

Day 2: Dozens of players of every age discovered the game, with new fans arriving throughout the day.

Day 3: Players sought out the table after finding Coffee Time in the event program. The team closed the weekend celebrating the creativity of fellow board-game designers.

March 2023 · First milestone

The first 100 games.

“We sold our first 100 Coffee Time games thanks to you. If we can make it in our hometown of Davis, we can make it anywhere!”

One of those first games traveled all the way to a coffee farm in Curití, Colombia—a fitting destination for the beginning of a global adventure.
