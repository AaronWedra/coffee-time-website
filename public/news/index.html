The next chapter

Coffee Time becomes a world you can enter.

The original tabletop adventure is expanding into a full-scale 3D video game. Coffee Time Adventures puts Little Miss Hot Shot at the center of a journey through five connected worlds filled with platforming, powers, strange machines and familiar faces.

A free public demo is available now while new environments, characters, music and game systems continue taking shape.

2026

Built in Davis

A collaborative Unreal Engine adventure

Club instructor and sound designer Nick Domke leads the 3D open-world project, with Milly Wedra voicing the feisty Little Miss Hot Shot. Students and collaborators can contribute world design, modeling, animation, enemies, interfaces, storytelling, sound and gameplay systems.

The goal is bigger than polishing one demo: it is to grow a universe through curiosity, shared creativity and hands-on game development.

2025

Convention diary

Back at San Diego Comic-Con

Coffee Time returned to the legendary convention surrounded by established creators and first-time game makers. More than 600 copies of the original board game were already being played by families, fans and nonprofits across the country and overseas.

NOV 2024

Convention diary

Invited to San Diego International Comic-Con

Day 1: A slow opening transformed into nonstop, back-to-back battles between coffee heroes and tea villains.

Day 2: Dozens of players of every age discovered the game, with new fans arriving throughout the day.

Day 3: Players sought out the table after finding Coffee Time in the event program. The team closed the weekend celebrating the creativity of fellow board-game designers.

March 2023 · First milestone

The first 100 games.

“We sold our first 100 Coffee Time games thanks to you. If we can make it in our hometown of Davis, we can make it anywhere!”

One of those first games traveled all the way to a coffee farm in Curití, Colombia—a fitting destination for the beginning of a global adventure.
